**Shifting perspectives: A historical perspective to struggles and resilience** was presented as a keynote lecture during **Stop Hatred Now 2025: Ecosystems of hope and resilience.** Through it, I unpack how historical and contemporary conflicts are shaping a simultaneity of experience, and how could we rethink resilience in the times we live in.
