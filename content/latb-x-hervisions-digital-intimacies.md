A night of performances, workshops and music, responding to the influential works of Ed Atkins, exploring the relationship between technology and its influence on how we perceive ourselves, intimacy and loss.

Co-curated in collaboration with [HERVISIONS](https://www.hervisions.world/), in response to *[Ed Atkins](https://www.tate.org.uk/whats-on/tate-britain/ed-atkins)*, as part of [Digital Intimacies](https://www.tate.org.uk/whats-on/london/digital-intimacies).

<br/>

**Digital Intimacies** is a season exploring the living relationship between humanity and technology.

Full program and list of artists **[here](https://www.tate.org.uk/whats-on/tate-britain/late-at-tate-britain)**
